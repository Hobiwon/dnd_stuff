## Character 1 ##
Name: Henk Goreaxe
Class & Subclass: Zealot Barbarian
Race: Half-Orc
1.) What is one person that is dear to your character and why?
- Henk has a younger brother, Holg, who was killed when Henk accidentally summoned the patron god of Orcs—Gruumsh—during a battle his clan was losing… Henk was the only survivor on either side. Henk loved his little brother and is filled with deep regret about the incident.
2.) If things in life went differently for your character and they never became an Adventurer, what other life could they have lived instead?
- Henk is considered funny amongst his people (Orcs, being notoriously serious) and has always had lofty dreams of moving to the big city and becoming a stand up comedian.
3.) One secret you have (either ongoing with the current party, or in the past)
- Henk refers to his brother, Holg, frequently, but talks about him as though he was his dog. Calls him “little buddy” and stuff like that. He does this as a way to cope with the fact that his accidental summoning of Gruumsh during a religious fervor (he is a Zealot Barbarian) wiped out his whole clan, including his brother. Overall, he is keeping secret the fact that his ecstatic rages have a chance to summon Gruumsh (which is disastrous), and that he considers himself the reason for his clan’s destruction.


## Character 2 ##
Name: Fimble “Figg” Fizzbang
Class & Subclass: Armorer Artificer
Race: Rock Gnome
1.) What is one person that is dear to your character and why?
- Figg is an apprentice of an eccentric gnomish inventor named Professor Oglethorpe “Oggy” Obnoticus. He found Figg on the street and took him in, giving him a home and taught him to be an inventor.
2.) If things in life went differently for your character and they never became an Adventurer, what other life could they have lived instead?
- Figg loves using his learned trade to create toys for kids and often distributes them in clever ways. He would probably live his passion by opening a toy shop in a major city.
3.) One secret you have (either ongoing with the current party, or in the past)
- He was born with a congenital heart condition and Prof. Oggy replaced it with one of arcano-tech. He has to feed it (directly into his chest) with arcane magic once daily to keep it going. He tends to create arcane trinkets/toys to use for this purpose, because he seems to be affected by the nature of the items fed to it. The professor told him that if he overloads it, it could be disastrous and explode, but he is extremely private about his refueling and the fact that he doesn’t have a flesh and blood heart like others.
